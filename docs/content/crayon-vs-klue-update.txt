Crayon and Klue are the first two names that come up when a team starts shopping for competitive intelligence. Neither lists a price, and both are products your team logs into and adopts. There's a third shape now: the analyst lives inside the AI assistant you already use. Here's how Crayon and Klue differ, who each one suits, and where Ripplewatch fits. A note up front: we make Ripplewatch, so weigh what we say about it accordingly. Crayon and Klue are described from what their own sites say, checked on October 14, 2026.

## What Crayon does

[Crayon](https://crayon.co) pairs automated competitor monitoring with battlecards deployed straight into Salesforce and Slack, where reps already work. Sparks, its AI feature, runs scheduled passes over competitor news, social activity and PR. The practical strength is distribution: the battlecard shows up where the deal is, not in a tool someone has to remember. See [how Ripplewatch compares to Crayon](/compare/crayon).

## What Klue does

[Klue](https://klue.com) is built around a Compete Agent that continuously gathers intel and pushes deal-specific insight to reps, a Win-Loss Suite that runs verified buyer interviews (including an AI interviewer for voice conversations), and Ask Klue, a chat interface for querying competitive data on demand. The buyer interviews are the deepest piece. Real, structured feedback from actual buyers is something most tools in this category don't attempt. See [how Ripplewatch compares to Klue](/compare/klue).

## Both are moving into AI assistants too

Crayon announced an [MCP server](https://www.crayon.co/blog/crayon-launches-first-competitive-intelligence-mcp-server) in September 2025, so its content can be used from tools like ChatGPT, Glean and Copilot. Klue's homepage shows questions you can ask, and battlecards you can update, from an assistant such as Claude, plus a Glean integration. In both cases what the assistant draws on is the compete content and the program you run inside their platform. That is a different job from starting with the assistant and adding competitor tracking to it.

## Crayon vs. Klue: which one fits

- Pick Crayon if your reps live in Salesforce and Slack and the main job is keeping battlecards current and in front of them.
- Pick Klue if verified buyer interviews are the thing you're missing and someone can run that program.
- Expect a sales cycle with either. Neither publishes pricing, so the first real number comes after a call.
- Both are set up for a team with dedicated implementation support or a CI or enablement owner.
