## Key findings

- We looked at the websites of 288 early-stage US software companies (25 or fewer employees).
- 56% (160) had no pricing page we could find. 17% (48) only say "contact sales". 22% (63) publish a price.
- Of the 29 companies with a monthly starting price we could read, the median was $129 a month. The range ran from $4.99 to $7,000.
- We hand-checked 20 of the "no pricing page" companies. 19 held up.
- If most of your competitors don't publish a price, a price change can't show up on a page you watch.
