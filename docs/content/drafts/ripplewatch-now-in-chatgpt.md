Ripplewatch has worked in Claude for a while. ChatGPT was the harder one. You had to add a custom connector by hand, which is a lot to ask of someone who just wants to try a thing. It's now in ChatGPT's plugin directory, so adding it looks like adding anything else.

If you're new here: Ripplewatch watches the competitors you pick and gives each one a Momentum score, which is just Heating up, Steady, Cooling or Gone quiet, along with the signals behind it. Then it checks each move against your own won and lost deals. A competitor's price cut only matters if it lines up with the way you've been losing. You ask in the chat you already have open, and there's no dashboard to remember to check.

## Adding it

Open [Ripplewatch in ChatGPT's plugin directory](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_6ac7c45cb79081918cdf5646ef1c3031), add it, and sign in to Ripplewatch when it asks. You approve the connection and you're done. You do need a Ripplewatch Connect account first, which is $29 a month plus usage you prepay for.

If your ChatGPT workspace needs an admin to approve plugins, or you'd rather set it up by hand, the [setup page](https://www.ripplewatch.ai/connect/claude-and-chatgpt) has the manual steps.

## What to ask first

Start with "Using Ripplewatch: give me my competitive briefing for this week." It tells you what changed and which competitor deserves your attention. Then try "which of my competitors is heating up, and why?" and you'll see the Momentum read with what's pulling it that way.

The one that gets better over time is "what is costing us deals, and what should we do about it?" It needs deals to work with, so tell it how a few went, in plain words. "We lost to Dave on price, they had a free tier" is enough. If you've got a spreadsheet of them, hand it that instead.

It reads public information about the competitors you choose, plus whatever you tell it. It can't see anyone's private data, and it can't touch your billing or team settings. You can disconnect it whenever you like.
