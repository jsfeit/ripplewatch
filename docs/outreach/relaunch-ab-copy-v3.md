# Relaunch campaign v3: the full picture (A) vs never miss a change (B), free read as the sneak peek

This is what is in the Instantly draft "Relaunch v3 - free read, no icebreaker (A question vs B time)". Not launched.

## The test

One product, two reasons to care.

- **A: the full picture.** Hypothesis: a competitor's moves are scattered across pricing pages, job boards, launches, press, reviews and your own deals. People will respond to a tool that pulls it all together, inside the assistant they already use, so one question gets everything. This is the integration and "answer in Claude or ChatGPT" angle.
- **B: nothing slips by.** Hypothesis: the stronger motive is not having to check by hand and not being surprised. This is the time-savings and "don't miss changes" angle you said we sell.

What we measure, in order of how much it tells us: free reads started per email (UTM `relaunch-v3-a` vs `relaunch-v3-b`), then replies, then paid signups. At 15 a day and a few hundred leads, treat it as directional.

What it can and can't tell us: the two arms differ in the whole angle (subject line, opening, what the product is "about"), not one word, so it teaches which framing pulls, not which sentence. Instantly also picks A or B separately at each step, so a lead can see A then B; the UTM on the link that actually got clicked is the cleaner signal than "which variant a lead is in".
