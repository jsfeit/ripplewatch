## 1. Blog post (publish in the admin blog editor)

**Title:** Ripplewatch is now in ChatGPT
**Meta description:** You can now ask ChatGPT who's heating up in your market and what's costing you deals. Add Ripplewatch from the plugin directory.

Until now you could use Ripplewatch in Claude, or in ChatGPT if you were willing to set up a custom connector by hand. Today it's in ChatGPT's plugin directory, so adding it takes a couple of clicks.

If you haven't seen it: Ripplewatch watches the competitors you pick, gives each one a Momentum score (Heating up, Steady, Cooling or Gone quiet), and checks every move against your own won and lost deals. The answer shows up in the chat you already have open, not in another dashboard.

**How to add it**

1. Open [Ripplewatch in ChatGPT's plugin directory](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_6ac7c45cb79081918cdf5646ef1c3031) and add it.
2. Sign in to Ripplewatch and approve the connection. You'll need a Ripplewatch Connect account, which is $29 a month plus usage you prepay for.
3. Ask your first question.

**Three things to ask first**

- "Using Ripplewatch: which of my competitors is heating up, and why?"
- "Using Ripplewatch: give me my competitive briefing for this week."
- "Using Ripplewatch: what is costing us deals, and what should we do about it?"

The third one gets sharper the more deals you tell it about. You can log a deal in plain words ("we lost to Acme on price, they had a free tier"), or hand it a CSV.

**What it can't do.** It reads public information about the competitors you choose, plus what you tell it. It can't see anyone's private data, and it can't change your billing or team settings. You can disconnect it any time.

If you'd rather add it by hand, or your ChatGPT workspace needs an admin to approve it, the [setup page](https://www.ripplewatch.ai/connect/claude-and-chatgpt) has the manual steps.
