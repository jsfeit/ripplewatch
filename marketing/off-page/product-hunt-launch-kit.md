## Description (the main launch post body)

Every competitive intelligence tool has the same first move: watch pricing pages, job boards, and news for changes, then tell you about it. That part's solved. The part nobody solves is the next question — does this actually matter to *my* business?

We built Ripplewatch to answer that question directly. You tell it about your positioning, your ICP, and the real reasons you've lost deals or lost customers — once. From then on, every signal we find (a pricing change, a new senior hire, a funding round, a bad review, an SEO shift) gets checked against that context and scored High, Medium, or Low, with the reasoning spelled out in plain English. The same pricing cut can be a High-relevance alert for one company and a Low for another, because it's scored against what actually threatens *your* deals, not a generic severity scale.

A few things we think are worth trying:
- **Ask** — query your own competitor data directly ("has anyone dropped pricing in the last month?"), scoped to your competitors and the last 90 days of signals.
- **Real reasoning, not just a score** — every alert explains *why* it's High/Medium/Low, tied back to your own positioning or a specific lost-deal reason.
- **Transparent, published pricing** — three self-serve tiers starting at $69/mo, no "contact sales" wall, no free tier padded with limitations (every signal is a real AI analysis call, so a watered-down free tier felt dishonest).

Would genuinely love feedback from anyone who's tried to do this with spreadsheets and Google Alerts before — that's exactly the problem this started as.

## Timing notes
- Product Hunt launches perform best posted 12:01am PT.
- Have 3-5 people ready to comment with genuine questions in the first hour (not just upvotes) — early comment activity matters more than raw upvote count for the algorithm.
- Don't ask people to upvote directly in outreach messages — Product Hunt's guidelines prohibit vote manipulation language; ask them to "check it out and leave feedback" instead.
