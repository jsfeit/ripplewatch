# PR / journalist pitch template

For pitching startup/SaaS/martech press (TechCrunch startup desk, SaaStr blog, MarTech.org, The SaaS News, local startup press, relevant newsletters). Personalize the opening line per outlet — never send this verbatim to more than one contact at the same publication.

---

**Subject line options (A/B, keep under 60 characters):**
- A founder-led take on why competitive intel tools miss the point
- Built the competitive intel tool I wished existed at 3 past jobs

**Body:**

Hi [Name],

I'm Jeremy, founder of Ripplewatch — reaching out because [specific reason tied to their beat: "you've covered a few competitive-intelligence tools this year" / "your piece on X resonated with a problem I built a company around"].

Quick pitch: most competitive intelligence tools solve monitoring — watching competitor pricing, hiring, and news for changes. Almost none of them solve relevance — telling you whether a specific change actually threatens *your* business, versus every other company using the same tool.

Ripplewatch scores every competitor signal against a customer's own positioning, ICP, and real lost-deal/churn reasons, rather than a generic priority scale. The same pricing cut can be flagged High for one company and Low for another.

A few things that might be a fit for a story:
- The founder story is genuine, not manufactured: I tracked competitors manually (spreadsheet, saved searches) across every job before this, and built the tool I wished existed.
- We publish real, self-serve pricing (starting at $69/mo) in a category that's mostly "contact sales" — happy to talk about why.
- We did an honest comparison of every competitor-tracking tool under $500/mo, including naming where they're genuinely better than us: [link to /blog/competitor-tracking-tools-under-500-a-month]

Happy to do a quick call, send more detail, or just answer questions over email — whatever's easiest. No pressure either way.

Thanks for your time,
Jeremy Feit
Founder, Ripplewatch
jeremy@ripplewatch.ai · ripplewatch.ai

---

## Notes on angle selection

Pick ONE of these angles per pitch — don't try to cover all three in one email:

1. **Founder story angle** — solo/small founder building a real tool out of a genuine, personally-felt problem. Best for founder-focused outlets and newsletters.
2. **Pricing transparency angle** — competitive intelligence is a category full of "contact sales" enterprise pricing; Ripplewatch is fully self-serve and published. Best for SaaS/pricing-strategy-focused writers.
3. **Honest-competitor-review angle** — the /blog/competitor-tracking-tools-under-500-a-month post is a genuinely differentiated hook (a vendor publicly reviewing competitors, including their strengths) that most outlets find more interesting than a standard launch pitch.

## Follow-up cadence
- Wait 5-7 business days before a single, short follow-up ("just floating this back to the top of your inbox — happy to send more detail if useful").
- Do not follow up more than once without a new hook (a launch, a press mention, new data).
