## G2

**Product name:** Ripplewatch

**Tagline (under 70 characters):**
Competitive intelligence scored to your business, not a feed

**Short description (under 200 characters, used in search results):**
Ripplewatch monitors your competitors' pricing, hiring, and news, then scores every finding against your own positioning and win/loss history — so you see what matters, not everything.

**Long description:**
Most competitive intelligence tools give you a feed: every pricing change, every job posting, every mention, all treated the same. Ripplewatch is built around a different idea — that the same signal can matter enormously to one company and mean nothing to another, depending on your positioning, your ICP, and the actual reasons you've lost deals or lost customers.

You tell Ripplewatch about your business once: your positioning, your ICP, your lost-deal and churn reasons. From there, every competitor signal — a pricing change, a new hire, a funding round, a review, an SEO shift — gets checked against that context and scored High, Medium, or Low, with the reasoning spelled out. Instead of a firehose you have to triage yourself, your team gets a short list of things worth acting on, with an explanation of why.

Alerts arrive in Slack and email as they happen. An "Ask" feature lets you query your own competitor data directly — scoped to your competitors, your positioning, and the last 90 days of signals — instead of digging through a dashboard.

Three tiers, all self-serve with published pricing: Starter ($69/mo, 3 competitors), Plus ($149/mo, up to 7 competitors, full relevance scoring, HubSpot pull), and Advanced ($349/mo, up to 20 competitors, onboarding support, Zoom call insights). No free tier — every signal is genuinely analyzed by AI as it's found, which has a real per-signal cost, so we didn't want to build a "free" tier that's actually just a worse product. 30-day money-back guarantee on every plan.

**Categories to select:** Competitive Intelligence, Market Intelligence, Sales Intelligence

**G2 Q&A prompts (draft answers):**

- *What does Ripplewatch do?* Monitors your competitors' pricing, hiring, product changes, news, and reviews, then scores each finding for relevance to your specific business instead of delivering an undifferentiated feed.
- *Who is Ripplewatch for?* Startup product, marketing, and sales teams who need to track a handful of real competitors without hiring a dedicated analyst or paying enterprise CI pricing.
- *What makes Ripplewatch different from other options?* The scoring is personalized — computed against your own positioning, ICP, and win/loss history — rather than a generic priority or severity scale applied the same way to every account.
